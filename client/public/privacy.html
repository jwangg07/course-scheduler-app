Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

UBCSchedules ("the app," "we," "our") is a free tool that helps UBC students build conflict free course schedules. This page explains what data is collected when you use the app and why.

Summary

UBCSchedules does not require an account, does not sell data, and does not store your course selections on our servers. The only data collected is (1) information you voluntarily submit through the bug report form.

1. Course Data & Schedule Generation

When you search for or add a course, your request is sent to our backend (hosted on Render), which queries UBC's own public Drupal JSON:API for section data (times, instructional method, enrollment status, etc.).

  • We cache this course/section data temporarily (server-side, in memory) to reduce load on UBC's servers. This cache contains only public course information.
  • Your generated schedules are computed entirely in your browser and are not sent to or stored on our servers.
  • We do not currently persist your selected courses or schedules between visits (no accounts, no server-side saving, no browser storage of your selections).

2. Bug Reports

If you choose to submit a bug report, you'll provide:

  • Your email address
  • A description of the issue

This information is sent via Resend, a transactional email service, directly to the project maintainer's inbox, so we can follow up if needed. It is used only for the purpose of investigating and responding to the reported issue and is not used for marketing or shared with third parties beyond Resend acting as our email-delivery provider. See Resend's Privacy Policy for how they process this data in transit.

Submitting a bug report is entirely optional, nothing is collected unless you fill out and submit the form.

3. Rate Limiting

To keep the app available and prevent abuse, our backend applies rate limiting based on IP address (via Express middleware). IP addresses are used transiently for this purpose only and are not logged or stored beyond what's needed to enforce request limits.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

Service Purpose Data involved
Render Backend hosting IP address (rate limiting only), no personal data stored
UBC Drupal JSON:API Source of course/section data No personal data sent; public course queries only
Resend Sends bug report emails Email address + message you provide, only if you submit a report

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes (e.g. if we add accounts, saved schedules, or new analytics). The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through the in-app bug report form, or via the project's GitHub repository.
